To make the "withdrawal" system work without you paying from your own pocket, you must understand a fundamental rule of game development: **The game itself must be profitable before it can pay its players.**

If you allow players to withdraw money without the game generating any income, you will eventually be forced to pay from your own pocket, or the system will fail.

Here is the professional approach to making this sustainable:

### 1. The Economic Reality (How to Fund Payouts)
If you want to offer real-money withdrawals, your game must act like a business. It needs revenue streams. To pay players without using your own money, you have to collect money from other sources first. Here are the three most common ways to do this:

*   **Advertising (The most common start):** You integrate ads (like Google AdSense or Unity Ads). Players watch a 30-second video to get "Bonus Energy" or "Extra Credits." The ad companies pay *you* for those views, and you use a portion of that money to fund the prize pool for withdrawals.
*   **In-App Purchases (Premium items):** Players can play for free, but they can pay real money to buy special "Skins," "Legendary Ships," or "Instant Refuel" packages. You keep 70–80% of that revenue, and use a portion to fund the withdrawal pool.
*   **Sponsorships/Partnerships:** Once your game has thousands of active players, companies may pay you to feature their products in your game (e.g., a "Sponsored Mission").

**My Advice:** Do not build the "Withdrawal" feature until the game is actually making money. Focus on building the **fun** first. If the game isn't fun, no one will play it, regardless of the money.

### 2. Developing the "Interstellar Mercenary" Theme
Let’s make the game so enjoyable that people play for the "Mission" experience, not just for the money. This will build the player base you need to eventually turn on monetization.

#### The Command Center (The Dashboard)
This is your Admin/User hub. Instead of just showing stats, make it look like a ship's navigation screen.

*   **Layout:** Dark blue/purple background, glowing green/orange text, and a "Sector Map."
*   **The Mission Board:** This is the "Collecting Missions" feature you asked for.

#### How to build the "Mission" logic
In your `combat_engine.py`, you can define a `Mission` class:

```python
class Mission:
    def __init__(self, name, difficulty, reward, duration):
        self.name = name          # e.g., "Escort Supply Run"
        self.difficulty = difficulty # 1-5 stars
        self.reward = reward      # Credits (Gold)
        self.duration = duration  # How long it takes
```

**The Gameplay Loop:**
1.  **Accept:** Player visits the "Milky Way Military Base" (The Mission Board).
2.  **Dispatch:** Player "sends" their ship on the mission. (You can create a `wait` time where they cannot use that ship).
3.  **Complete:** After the duration, the player returns to the game to "Claim Reward" and get their Credits (Gold).
